Rules

Black & White
  • 9 white, 9 black and the red queen. Player 1 plays white, Player 2 plays black.
  • Pocket one of your coins and you shoot again. A coin of the other colour counts for the other player and ends your turn.
  • Queen: pocket it, then cover it by pocketing one of your coins in the same shot or the next one. If you fail, the queen goes back to the centre.
  • Foul (your striker goes in a pocket): your turn ends, the coins from that shot go back, and one of your pocketed coins goes back too. If you have none, you owe it.
  • Your last coin before the queen is covered is a foul: it goes back with one more coin.
  • You win when all 9 of your coins are pocketed and the queen is covered.
Freestyle
  • Any coin counts for the player who pockets it: white 20, black 10, queen 50 (the queen must be covered by any coin).
  • First to the target points wins.
No Queen: the red coin is removed.
